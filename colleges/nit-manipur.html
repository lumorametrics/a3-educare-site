About National Institute of Technology Manipur

National Institute of Technology Manipur began in 2010 from a temporary campus at a government polytechnic in Takyelpat, Imphal. Its permanent campus is at Langol, and the institute's profile says it has acquired 341.5 acres.

It offers B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc. and Ph.D. programmes, and was declared an Institute of National Importance under a 2012 Act.

01

Academic Areas & Programs

  • B.Tech: Civil, Mechanical, Computer Science, Electronics and Communication, and Electrical Engineering
  • Postgraduate: M.Tech and M.Sc.
  • Doctoral: Ph.D.
02

Admissions

B.Tech places come through JEE (Main) and JoSAA, M.Tech through GATE and CCMT, M.Sc. through JAM and CCMN, and Ph.D. through a written test and interview.

Candidates must meet the conditions of the relevant entrance exam and counselling process for the admission year.

Entrance exams: JEE (Main) for B.Tech; GATE for M.Tech; JAM for M.Sc.

03

Recognition & Affiliation

NIT Manipur is an Institute of National Importance under a 2012 Act.

Affiliation: National Institute of Technology under the NIT Act, 2007

04

Campus & Facilities

The permanent campus is at Langol, Imphal, on land of 341.5 acres according to the institute's profile.

05

Hostel

The institute's 2021-22 prospectus refers to boys' and girls' hostels and says more will open on campus in coming years. Listings differ on the current number.
