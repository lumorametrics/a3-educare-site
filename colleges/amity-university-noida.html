About Amity University Noida

Amity University Noida, formally part of Amity University Uttar Pradesh (AUUP), was established in 2005 through the Amity University Uttar Pradesh Act. The university's Noida campus is located in Sector 125, Noida, Uttar Pradesh.

The university offers a broad portfolio of academic programmes through its various schools and institutes. Its current programme listings include areas such as engineering and technology, computer science and IT, management, commerce, law, biotechnology, sciences, architecture, design, psychology, humanities, education, health sciences, pharmacy and other specialised disciplines.

Amity University states that it is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). Its official accreditation information also lists NAAC accreditation and international quality/accreditation affiliations.

The Noida campus provides academic infrastructure along with laboratories, libraries, classrooms, sports facilities, dining facilities, accommodation and other student-support services.

For students considering admission, programme-specific eligibility, selection procedures, fees and other conditions should be checked on the official programme page before applying because these requirements can differ between courses.

02

Admissions

Admission at Amity University Noida is conducted through the university's application and programme-specific selection process.

Students generally need to:

  1. Select the desired programme.
  2. Check the programme-specific eligibility criteria.
  3. Complete the online application form.
  4. Upload the required academic and personal documents.
  5. Pay the applicable application fee.
  6. Complete the selection process prescribed for the programme.
  7. Follow the university's admission instructions for document verification and fee payment after selection.

The university's current programme pages specify selection procedures for individual programmes. Depending on the programme, selection may involve an application-based assessment, video responses, interview or other programme-specific requirements.

Students should always check the current programme page and admission guidelines before submitting an application.

Eligibility depends on the programme selected.

For many undergraduate programmes, candidates are required to have passed Class XII or an equivalent examination from a recognised board, while specific programmes may require particular subjects and minimum marks.

For postgraduate programmes, candidates generally need the relevant undergraduate qualification and must satisfy the programme-specific eligibility conditions.

For example, Amity's current programme listings show programme-specific requirements such as Class XII aggregate marks and subject combinations for several engineering and computer-related programmes.

Important: Eligibility should be verified separately for the selected course because requirements can differ substantially between programmes.

Entrance exams: Programme-specific Amity University Noida does not have one single entrance requirement applicable to every programme. Selection requirements vary by course. Depending on the programme, the admission process may consider academic performance and/or university-prescribed selection procedures. Some programmes may also consider scores or qualifications from relevant external examinations where applicable. Students should check the official programme page for the exact selection criteria before applying.

03

Recognition & Affiliation

Amity University states that its universities and degrees are recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC). The university's official accreditation information also states that Amity University Uttar Pradesh has received NAAC accreditation with A+ grade and lists international quality/accreditation recognition including WASC and QAA.

For individual professional programmes, students should separately check the programme-specific regulatory recognition or approval wherever applicable.

Affiliation: Amity University Uttar Pradesh (AUUP) Amity University Uttar Pradesh is established under a State Act of Uttar Pradesh. It operates as a university rather than as an affiliated college.

04

Fees

Fees at Amity University Noida are programme-specific and may differ according to the course, specialisation and applicable fee category.

The university's official programme listings publish programme-wise fee information. For example, current programme pages show different first-year semester fee figures across programmes, demonstrating that there is no single fee applicable to all courses.

Students should check the official programme page for the latest tuition fee, applicable academic charges, security deposit and other fees before taking admission.

The university's admission FAQ also states that a one-time academic security deposit of ₹20,000 is payable at admission

Amity University Noida's campus provides facilities designed to support academic, professional and extracurricular development.

Major facilities include:

  • Sports facilities
  • Sports courts and playing arenas

The university's official infrastructure page describes the Noida campus as having classrooms, hi-tech laboratories, libraries, sports complexes, restaurants, bookstores and on-campus hostels.

06

Hostel

Amity University Noida provides hostel accommodation for students on campus.

The university states that separate accommodation facilities are available for boys and girls. Hostel options include air-conditioned residential apartment-style accommodation as well as non-air-conditioned rooms, subject to availability and applicable rules.

The university describes its residential facilities as including rooms, attached bathrooms and common living facilities, along with security and wardens.

Hostel availability, room categories and charges may vary, so students should confirm the current hostel details directly with the university.

07

Placements

Amity University Noida has a dedicated Corporate Resource Centre that coordinates placement activities, industry interactions and career preparation.

According to the university's current placement data, 6,000+ students were placed in 2024–25, with 800+ recruiting companies, a reported ₹75 lakh highest package, and 4,500+ pre-placement offers. These figures are university-reported and may vary by programme and graduating batch.

The university also describes placement preparation activities including resume development, aptitude and technical preparation, soft-skills training, mock interviews, group discussions and industry interaction.

Students should evaluate placement data according to their specific programme rather than relying only on university-wide figures.

08

Scholarships

Amity University Noida offers several forms of scholarship and financial assistance.

The university's scholarship information mentions support based on factors such as:

  • Academic merit
  • Financial need
  • Sports achievement
  • Arts and other talent
  • Differently-abled student provisions
  • Other scholarship categories as applicable

Scholarship rules, eligibility, availability and continuation requirements can change by academic session and programme. The university advises applicants to check the applicable scholarship conditions before applying.
